VU Buddy Privacy Policy

Effective 5 September 2026. Developer: Ar Gorsi.

VU Buddy is an unofficial Chrome extension for students of the Virtual University of Pakistan. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or part of the Virtual University of Pakistan.

What the extension does

While you are already signed in to VULMS in Chrome, the extension reads the To Do Calendar and course names, stores upcoming assignment / quiz / GDB deadlines on your computer, shows them in a popup, and can send Chrome reminders before they are due.

Data stored on your device

Deadlines, course codes, course names, reminder flags, and sync status are stored only in Chrome’s local extension storage on this browser profile.

The extension does not store your VULMS username, password, roll number, cookies, or session tokens.

Network

The only network destination is https://vulms.vu.edu.pk, to fetch the calendar and home page using the VULMS session Chrome already has. The extension never reads cookie values.

There is no VU Buddy server, account, analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or CDN.

What we do not collect

The developer does not receive your VULMS data, browsing history, or identifiers. Nothing is sold or used for advertising.

Permissions

  • storage — keep deadlines on this device
  • alarms — refresh the calendar and check reminders while the worker is asleep
  • notifications — 72-hour, 24-hour, and 2-hour reminders
  • https://vulms.vu.edu.pk/* — read the calendar and home page you are already using

Contact

Use the Chrome Web Store listing’s support link for VU Buddy, published by Ar Gorsi.

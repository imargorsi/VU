VU Deadlines Privacy Policy

Effective 5 September 2026. Developer: Ar Gorsi.

VU Deadlines is an unofficial Chrome extension for students of the Virtual University of Pakistan. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or part of the Virtual University of Pakistan.

What the extension does

While you are already signed in to VULMS in Chrome, the extension reads the To Do Calendar and course names, stores upcoming assignment / quiz / GDB deadlines on your computer, shows them in a popup, and can send Chrome reminders before they are due (72 hours, 24 hours, 2 hours, plus one daily digest after 08:00 Pakistan time).

Data stored on your device

Deadlines, course codes, course names, reminder flags, sync status, and a last-digest date are stored only in Chrome’s local extension storage on this browser profile.

The extension does not store your VULMS username, password, roll number, cookies, or session tokens.

Network

The only network destination is https://vulms.vu.edu.pk, to fetch the calendar and home page using the VULMS session Chrome already has. The extension never reads cookie values.

A content script runs only on https://vulms.vu.edu.pk/*. It is a fallback fetch in that page’s origin if the background worker cannot see your existing session. It does not run on other sites and does not inject ads or trackers.

There is no VU Deadlines server, account, analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or CDN.

What we do not collect

The developer does not receive your VULMS data, browsing history, or identifiers. Nothing is sold, used for advertising, or shared with third parties.

How to delete your data

Remove the extension from Chrome. That deletes the local cache on this profile. You can also clear it with Chrome’s “Clear browsing data” for extension storage, or by removing the unpacked / installed item.

Permissions

  • storage — keep deadlines on this device
  • alarms — refresh the calendar and check reminders while the worker is asleep
  • notifications — deadline reminders and the daily digest
  • https://vulms.vu.edu.pk/* — read the calendar and home page you are already using

Contact

Use the Chrome Web Store listing’s support link for VU Deadlines, published by Ar Gorsi. Privacy questions about this extension can also be sent through that listing.
